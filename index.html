What this is

A census of international amateur free agents signed in the 2009-10 through 2025 signing periods, built from MLB's public transaction log rather than from published signing lists. Every rate on this page is a share of the signings that log records. None is an official figure, and none can be: a signing the log never recorded is not in it.

How a player qualifies

Signed with an MLB organization, never appeared in a Rule 4 draft, no professional experience in any league before signing, and under 23 at signing. Players born in the US, Canada or Puerto Rico are excluded only at 17.5 and above, since draft eligibility follows residency rather than birthplace.

Known limits

Signing bonus figures exist for about a fifth of the census and are skewed to publicized deals, and a partial bonus column would read as zero. Dates of birth are as reported; age falsification is documented in this market. Some signings have no record of their own and reach the census only through a later assignment.

Provenance

Rows marked ◇ had no signing transaction logged; the period is inferred from the player's first rookie-league assignment and age is measured at the period open. Everything else is an observed signing record.
